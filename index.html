WELCOME TO THE MAXIMUM TIME FRAME

Your certification journey continues here.

Graduation is a milestone, not the finish line. Your Maximum Time Frame (MTF) is a designated period after graduation to continue preparing for and taking certification exams. This portal brings your preparation, support, and important procedures together in one place.

WHY MTF MATTERS

Make the most of your next opportunity.

Industry certifications can help demonstrate your technical knowledge. Consistent preparation, practice, and instructor guidance can help you approach your exam with greater confidence.

Your MTF success checklist

  1. Know your goal. Select the certification you're preparing for and review its study plan.
  2. Build a routine. Use training platforms, boot camps, and practice resources regularly.
  3. Ask questions early. Schedule a 1-on-1 when you need help understanding an exam objective.
  4. Know the policies. Review voucher requirements and exam rescheduling rules before testing.

Pay "IT" Forward MTF Expired? What's Next?

What is the MTF?

The MTF (Maximum Time Frame) period is a designated timeframe after a student graduates to continue preparing for and taking their certification exams!

MTF Voucher Limitations

Please keep the following critical policy limitations in mind:

  • You can only have ONE active voucher at a time.
  • Students are approved for up to a 4th attempt at an exam.
  • Any certification voucher requested must be completed by the student's designated MTF expiration date.

Exam Rescheduling & Pearson VUE Support

Important: This information applies only to existing exam appointments, rescheduling, cancellations, and technical issues. It does NOT apply to new exam attempts or requests for additional exam vouchers.

For new exam attempts, students must schedule a 1-on-1 appointment with their MTF Instructor and follow the instructions provided before a new voucher can be approved.

Rescheduling Your Exam

  • Exams must be rescheduled at least 24 hours before your scheduled appointment to avoid forfeiting your voucher.
  • A forfeited voucher may count as one of your allotted exam attempts, even if you did not take the exam.
  • Always verify your new exam date and time through your Pearson VUE confirmation email.

Rescheduling Your Exam (Due to Testing Center or Pearson VUE Cancellation)

  • If your exam is canceled by the testing center or Pearson VUE, you should be able to reschedule using your original voucher, provided it remains valid.
  • Once your exam has been rescheduled, verify the new date and time in your Pearson VUE confirmation email.
  • Notify your MTF Instructor immediately of any exam cancellations, rescheduling, or interruptions.

Pearson VUE Technical Support

Phone: 877-551-7587

Contact Pearson VUE if you experience:

  • Issues completing the exam check-in process.
  • A testing center that is closed upon arrival.
  • Testing center closures due to severe weather.
  • Being stuck on the online exam queue screen.
  • Technical issues, disconnections, or exam crashes.
  • Issues connecting with your online proctor.

Important Reminders

  • Contact Pearson VUE immediately if an issue prevents you from testing.
  • Request and retain a support case number for any reported issues.
  • Notify your MTF Instructor immediately of any exam cancellations, rescheduling, or interruptions.
  • New exam attempts require MTF Instructor approval. Schedule a 1-on-1 appointment and follow all instructions provided. Do not use these rescheduling procedures to request a new exam attempt.

MTF Expired? What's Next?

If your MTF has expired and you are still interested in pursuing additional certification attempts, contact Alumni Services at alumni@mycomputercareer.com.

They can assist you with enrollment in a free, self-paced refresher course, and upon successful completion, you may be eligible to receive an additional exam voucher. Alumni Services can provide full details regarding this process.

We wish you the best in your continued certification journey, and please feel free to reach out if you have any questions.
