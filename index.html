Pay "IT" Forward MTF Expired? What's Next?

What is the MTF?

The MTF (Maximum Time Frame) period is a designated timeframe after a student graduates to continue preparing for and taking their certification exams!

MTF Voucher Limitations

Please keep the following critical policy limitations in mind:

  • You can only have ONE active voucher at a time.
  • Students are approved for up to a 4th attempt at an exam.
  • Any certification voucher requested must be completed by the student's designated MTF expiration date.

MTF Expired? What's Next?

If your MTF has expired and you are still interested in pursuing additional certification attempts, contact Alumni Services at alumni@mycomputercareer.com.

They can assist you with enrollment in a free, self-paced refresher course, and upon successful completion, you may be eligible to receive an additional exam voucher. Alumni Services can provide full details regarding this process.

We wish you the best in your continued certification journey, and please feel free to reach out if you have any questions.

Additional Recommendations

  • Schedule additional 1-on-1 sessions with a Lead Instructor whenever you need extra assistance or clarification.
  • Attend the weekly Boot Camps and Master Courses to reinforce key concepts, gain additional exam tips, and learn from instructor-led demonstrations.
  • Continue using MeasureUp practice exams throughout your preparation to reinforce concepts, identify knowledge gaps, and build exam confidence.
  • After each practice test, spend time reviewing every incorrect answer—not just the correct one. Understanding why an answer is correct is one of the most effective ways to prepare for the certification exam.
  • If possible, perform hands-on practice with hardware, networking equipment, virtualization software, and mobile devices whenever you study a related objective. Practical experience greatly improves knowledge retention and prepares you for Performance-Based Questions (PBQs).
